About

"No problem can withstand the assault of sustained thinking from multiple points of view." - Anonymous



 Hi, I am Tyler, also known as BraveCat. I have followed frontier science topics for years and built Frontier Signals as a dashboard and research hub for space weather, geophysics, historical data, models, articles, and related fields. I am not an expert; I am an independent researcher and enthusiast. The aim is to create a practical space for collecting public data, source material, and ideas that are useful for learning and comparison.
