Live earthquake data can be loaded from USGS, EMSC, GFZ/GEOFON, or a combined deduplicated multi-agency option. ISC is not listed because its event service does not allow browser-side CORS fetches.

VolcanoDiscovery supplies reported volcano activity flags. Red volcano markers indicate reported eruption, eruption warning, or unrest; check the report date and source. Orange markers show earthquake proximity only and do not predict an eruption. Gray markers do not establish inactivity. Static volcano locations are supplemented by source coordinates where matched. See the Volcano Monitoring page for detailed reports. Wildfires, floods, droughts, tropical systems, and Schumann resonance have moved to the Live Earth Monitor.
