# Tectonic plate boundaries

`PB2002_boundaries.json` is the unmodified GeoJSON from
https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates/blob/master/GeoJSON/PB2002_boundaries.json,
retrieved November 18, 2026.

Original model: Peter Bird (2003), *An updated digital model of plate boundaries*,
Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems 4(3), 1027, doi:10.1029/2001GC000252.
Conversion: Hugo Ahlenius, Nordpil; GeoJSON contributed by csterling.
